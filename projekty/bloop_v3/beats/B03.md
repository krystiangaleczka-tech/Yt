Narracja:
To understand the Bloop, start with a war that was fought by listening. In the 1960s, the US Navy laid underwater microphones across the oceans. The system was called SOSUS, the Sound Surveillance System. Its job was simple: hear a Soviet submarine long before anyone could see it.
