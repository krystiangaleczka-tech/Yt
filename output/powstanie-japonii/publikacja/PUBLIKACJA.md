**1 — o 1:14**
```
According to Japan's oldest chronicles, how was the first island made?
A) It rose on a giant turtle's back
B) It grew from a drop of salt off a jewelled spear ✓
C) It hatched from a dragon's egg
D) It fell from the sky as a star
Explanation: The gods Izanagi and Izanami stirred the sea with a jewelled spear. Salt dripped from its tip, and from that drop grew the first island.
```

**2 — o 2:19**
```
Which goddess hid in a cave and plunged heaven and earth into darkness?
A) Izanami
B) Amaterasu ✓
C) Uzume
D) Kushinada
Explanation: Amaterasu, the sun goddess, shut herself in a rock cave after her brother Susanoo wrecked her rice fields. Watch how the gods lure her out.
```

**3 — o 3:43**
```
How did the storm god Susanoo defeat the eight-headed serpent?
A) He froze it with snow
B) He got it drunk on sake ✓
C) He trapped it in a cave
D) He called down lightning
Explanation: He set out eight vats of strong sake. Every head drank and fell asleep, and in its tail Susanoo found a sword, one of the three sacred treasures of Japan.
```

**4 — o 6:54**
```
Japan's national football team has a crow on its shirts. Which crow is it?
A) Yatagarasu ✓
B) Tengu
C) Karasu-tengu
D) Kinshi
Explanation: In the chronicles, heaven sent the great crow Yatagarasu to guide Prince Iwarebiko, the future Emperor Jimmu, through the wild mountains of Kumano. Later it was painted with three legs.
```

**5 — o 10:52**
```
What does "Nihon", Japan's own name for itself, mean?
A) Land of islands
B) Eight great islands
C) Origin of the sun ✓
D) Land of the gods
Explanation: The court renamed the country Nihon in the late seventh century. Seen from China, that is where the sun rose.
```
